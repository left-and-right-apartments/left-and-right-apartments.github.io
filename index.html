        1. Please use the kitchen range hood while cooking.

        3. Leave the outside light on when you go out at night.
        4. Treat the home with care, as if it were your own.

        A Climate Resilience Fee set by the Greek government (law 5170/2025) applies to every stay. It replaces the former stay-over tax and supports Greece's natural-disaster fund.

        Getting around by taxi

        Need a taxi in Heraklion? Call Knossos Taxi or book online on their website. You can also schedule your ride for a specific day and time.

        Call +30 2810 211212 Book online

        Ride apps

        Please note that Uber and Bolt do not operate in Heraklion. The app for calling a taxi here is FREENOW.
